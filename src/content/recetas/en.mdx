recipes:
  - id: pan-de-muerto
    name: Pan de muerto
    description: A sweet, aromatic bread with orange blossom water and bone-shaped strips.
    ingredients:
      - name: Wheat flour
        amount: 500 g
      - name: Sugar
        amount: 100 g
      - name: Butter
        amount: 100 g
      - name: Eggs
        amount: "4"
      - name: Orange blossom water
        amount: 30 ml
    steps:
      - Mix flour, sugar, and yeast; add eggs, butter, and orange blossom water.
      - Knead until smooth, form round buns, and add bone-shaped dough strips.
      - Bake at 180 °C until golden, brush with butter, and sprinkle with sugar.

## Seasonal flavors

Día de Muertos cooking honors those who are gone: their favorite dishes — such as pan de muerto or tamales — are prepared, shared on the offering, and savored as a family.
